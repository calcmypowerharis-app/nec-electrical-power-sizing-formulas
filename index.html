Governing Equations & Code Standards

1. NEC Continuous Load & Circuit Breaker Sizing (NEC 210.20(A))

Under the National Electrical Code, any load energized for 3 hours or longer is designated as a continuous load. Overcurrent protection devices must not carry continuous loads exceeding 80% of their nameplate rating:

I_continuous_max = I_breaker_rating * 0.80
P_continuous_safe = V_circuit * I_breaker_rating * 0.80 * PF
I_breaker_required = I_continuous_load * 1.25
Circuit Rating Voltage Peak Breaker Wattage NEC 80% Continuous Limit Minimum Conductor
15 Amps 120V AC 1,800 Watts 1,440 Watts 14 AWG Copper
20 Amps 120V AC 2,400 Watts 1,920 Watts 12 AWG Copper
30 Amps 240V AC 7,200 Watts 5,760 Watts 10 AWG Copper
50 Amps 240V AC 12,000 Watts 9,600 Watts 6 AWG Copper
200 Amps 240V AC 48,000 Watts 38,400 Watts 2/0 Cu or 4/0 Al

2. Motor Locked-Rotor Starting Surge (NEC Article 430)

Compressors and electric pump motors draw inrush surge currents up to 6 times full-load amps. Portable and standby generators must support the total running wattage plus the single largest startup surge delta:

P_starting_delta = P_starting_peak - P_running
P_running_total = Sum(P_running_i)
P_surge_max_delta = Max(P_starting_delta_i)
P_generator_planning = (P_running_total + P_surge_max_delta) * 1.25

3. Battery Usable Energy & Inverter Runtime

To accurately model battery reserve time, convert Amp-hour capacity to nominal energy (Wh) and apply chemistry Depth of Discharge (DoD) limits and DC-to-AC conversion losses:

E_nominal_Wh = V_nominal_DC * Capacity_Ah
E_usable_Wh = E_nominal_Wh * DoD_factor * Inverter_Efficiency
Runtime_Hours = E_usable_Wh / P_AC_load_watts

4. Solar PV Array & Charge Controller Deratings (NEC Article 690)

Solar charge controllers must be sized for continuous array power output plus a 125% safety factor to handle solar irradiance spikes and cold temperature open-circuit voltage (Voc) elevation:

I_controller_nominal = P_array_STC / V_battery_nominal
I_controller_planning = I_controller_nominal * 1.25
Voc_cold = Voc_STC * [1 + Alpha_Voc * (T_min_record - 25)]

5. Conductor Voltage Drop (Ohm's Law & NEC 210.19(A))

Branch circuits should be engineered to limit voltage drop to 3% or less for optimal equipment operating efficiency:

Single-Phase AC / DC:   VD = 2 * I * (Length_ft / 1000) * R_conductor
Three-Phase Balanced:   VD = sqrt(3) * I * (Length_ft / 1000) * R_conductor
Voltage Drop Percent:   VD_percent = (VD / V_source) * 100
